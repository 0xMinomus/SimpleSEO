# Sub-Skill 08: Content Quality & Topic Clusters

Focus: Ensure every landing page satisfies real search intent, avoids keyword cannibalization, and stays scannable for both humans and LLMs.

## Checks

1. **Intent Mapping (1 Primary Intent per URL)**
   - Homepage: Brand overview, core value proposition, proof, pricing summary, FAQ.
   - Service/Vertical pages: Specific customer problem, deliverables, relevant case studies, direct CTA.
   - Pricing page: Clear tier comparison, inclusions, turnaround time, payment terms.
   - Portfolio page: Real live projects with category, tech stack, and tangible outcomes.

2. **Heading & Passage Structure**
   - Exactly one `<h1>` per page containing the primary keyword naturally.
   - Logical `<h2>` and `<h3>` hierarchy (never skip levels for visual styling).
   - Keep paragraphs concise and front-load clear answers inside the first 40 to 60 words of key sections.

3. **Zero Cannibalization & Zero Thin Pages**
   - Ensure no two pages share identical `<title>`, `<meta description>`, or duplicate hero copy.
   - Avoid empty placeholder pages or doorway pages stuffed with city names and identical boilerplate.
   - Cross-link sub-pages naturally inside related-services sections and breadcrumbs.
