# Sub-Skill 07: Local SEO, E-E-A-T & Off-Page Authority

Focus: Strengthen geographic relevance, genuine trust signals, and external brand authority without inventing fake claims.

## On-Site Signals

1. **Geographic Metadata**
   - Add `<meta name="geo.region" content="COUNTRY-REGION" />` and `<meta name="geo.placename" content="City, Country" />` matching the business's real base.
   - Populate `areaServed`, `address.addressRegion`, `address.addressCountry`, and `contactPoint` in JSON-LD (`LocalBusiness` / `ProfessionalService`).

2. **Authentic E-E-A-T Proof**
   - Link to real published portfolio projects, live demos, public developer/organization profiles (`sameAs`), real email, and direct contact channels.
   - Never generate fake customer counts, fabricated reviews, stock avatars labeled as clients, or unverified security badges.

## Off-Page Action Plan (Provided to User After Technical Deployment)

Explain clearly to the user which ranking factors live outside code and why they matter:
1. **Google Business Profile (GBP)**: Register exact brand name + primary service category + real service area + website URL + real portfolio photos to unlock Google Maps / Local Pack visibility.
2. **External Brand Mentions & Backlinks**: AI search engines weigh external brand citations heavily, and Google drops "Did you mean...?" spell-corrections once consistent brand mentions exist across LinkedIn, YouTube, GitHub profiles, business directories, and optional client footer credits pointing to the canonical URL.
3. **Real Client Reviews**: Ask satisfied customers for genuine Google Business Profile reviews mentioning the service provided and city/region.
