# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to keeping participation in **SimpleSEO** a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps create a healthy community include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Giving and accepting constructive technical feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what genuinely helps website owners and maintainers
- Showing empathy toward other contributors

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, trolling, insulting comments, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing) without explicit permission
- Submitting spam PRs, SEO black-hat tactics, or deceptive cloaking techniques
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including the GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, and discussions.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer at `beliweb@gmail.com`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
